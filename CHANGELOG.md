# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - not released yet

- A large splash in the middle of the screen the moment your hunter learns a
  pet ability from a tamed beast: the ability's icon in a gold ring, its name
  and rank, the pet's portrait, "Fluffy has taught you Claw." and Fluffy's
  free training points.
- Only abilities learned in the wild count, in every client language;
  abilities bought from a pet trainer do not.
- The splash plays the call of the teaching pet's family, a cat's growl or a
  wolf's howl (`/pal sound family|levelup|off`).
- Learned two at once? They show one after the other.
- When you summon or tame a pet, chat says which abilities it can still
  teach you (`/pal hints on|off`). Open Beast Training once so the addon
  knows your ranks.
- Everything you learned is kept per character: see it in the options panel
  or with `/pal history`.
- Optional screenshot of each splash (`/pal screenshot on`, off by default).
- Options panel under Esc > Options > AddOns with all settings and the
  history; `/pal` lists the commands.
- English, German, French, Spanish, Korean and Russian.
